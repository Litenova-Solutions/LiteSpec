# Tax Receipt Retention

## Summary

The national tax law requires a company to keep its business receipts for seven years.

## What It Requires

Expenses keeps every receipt of a reimbursed expense until the retention ends. Personal data past its need is erased, while the bare business facts stay for the tax file.
