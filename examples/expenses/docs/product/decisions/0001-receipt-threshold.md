# Receipt threshold

## Context

Small expenses need no proof, large ones do.

## Options

Zero, 25, or 75 EUR.

## Decision

Expenses above 25 EUR need a receipt.

## Consequences

Small expenses stay light to submit.

## Evidence

Finance review, February 2026.
