The cast is the one set of people, places and amounts that the Claimwick examples share. Harbor and Pine is the consultancy that uses the product, and its employees travel to clients and file the cost.

Ava is the employee who travels. Ben is her manager, and he approves her expenses. Cara works in finance, and she reimburses them. Each person's routine is in the record above, so an example never invents one.

The client visit (`occasion/client-visit`) is the occasion every example of a trip draws on. A train fare there (`figure/train-fare`) runs about 48.90 EUR, and the receipt threshold (`figure/receipt-threshold`) is 25 EUR.
