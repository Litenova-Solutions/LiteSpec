# Expenses Wait Weeks

## Who Has It

An employee who submitted an expense and waits for a decision while carrying the amount from their own account.

## What They Do Today

The employee emails the approver for a faster decision, and the approver answers from memory with no record of the wait.

## Evidence

The benchmark in research/approval-time-benchmarks shows small companies decide within one week, while Claimwick averages three.

## What Would Retire It

Each submitted expense is decided within five business days, and the employee sees the decision without asking.
