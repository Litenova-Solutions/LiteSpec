# Approval

## Who It Serves and Why

Employees submit work expenses and wait for the money, managers decide each one, and finance reimburses the approved ones. Expenses arrive daily, and each one needs a timely decision with proof where the policy asks for it.

## What It Offers

Every submitted expense is decided within five business days. Expenses above the receipt threshold carry a receipt. Nobody approves their own expense, and each approved expense is reimbursed at most once. The personal data of a decided expense is erased after its retention, and each month-end submission is acknowledged within two seconds. The receipt threshold comes from decision/0001-receipt-threshold, and the five-day target follows research/approval-time-benchmarks.

## What Good Looks Like

Ava submits a 48.90 EUR train fare with its receipt on Monday. Ben approves it on Tuesday. Cara reimburses it on Friday, and Ava sees the paid state in her list. Employees learn the outcome within days instead of weeks, and finance pays from a file it can defend.
