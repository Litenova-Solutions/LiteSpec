# Approval Time Benchmarks

This research asks how fast small companies decide expense claims. Its answer supports the 5-business-day target that the approval feature sets for Claimwick, and it says how sure the team can be of that target.

## Findings

Small companies decide expense claims within 1 week, according to one recent [industry study][example-expense-study]. Five business days is therefore a defensible target for Claimwick.

The study is small, so the team holds this finding with medium confidence. A second, larger study would raise it.
