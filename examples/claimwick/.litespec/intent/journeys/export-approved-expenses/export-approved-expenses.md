# Export Approved Expenses

Finance runs this journey once for each payout period. Finance collects the expenses that managers approved in the period into one batch, which the payout then reads. The batch holds one row for each approved expense, so the payout pays every approved expense of the period.

## Example

At the end of the week, Cara exports the approved expenses. Two fares were approved this week, including Ava's 48.90 EUR train fare, so the batch holds two rows, and Cara exports it once for the payout.
