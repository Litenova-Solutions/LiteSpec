# Decided in Days

## Why It Matters

Employees plan around reimbursements, and a late decision holds their money. A fast decision keeps trust in the whole flow.

## What Counts as Done

Every submitted expense is decided within five business days. The median hours from submission to decision stay under 120.

## What It Connects To

The submit-and-reimburse journey serves this goal, and the approval reminders feature advances it.
