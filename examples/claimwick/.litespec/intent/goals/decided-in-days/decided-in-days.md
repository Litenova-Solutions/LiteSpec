# Decided in Days

Claimwick is a product that moves work expenses from submission to payout. This goal sets how fast a manager must decide each submitted expense: within 5 business days. Employees plan around their reimbursements, so a late decision holds their money. A fast decision keeps their trust in the whole flow.

## Target

Every submitted expense is decided within 5 business days. The median time from submission to decision stays under 120 hours.

The 120-hour median sits beside the 5-day rule, and the two figures use different units. 120 hours is 5 calendar days of 24 hours each. The record does not say how weekends count in either figure, so the two figures may not agree.

## Measurement

The measure is the median number of hours from an expense's submission to its decision.

No measured baseline is recorded for this. The record also does not say which system or period the median is taken from.
