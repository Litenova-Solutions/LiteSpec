# Contractor Expenses

Contractors who work for the company pay work costs too, such as travel to a client, a test device or a conference ticket. Today they add these costs to their invoice by hand, and finance checks each line against a receipt sent by email. The owner wants Claimwick to take contractor expenses one day the way it takes employees' expenses, so one approval flow and one tax file cover both.

The direction covers submitting, approving and reimbursing a contractor's work costs. It does not cover paying the contractor's invoice for the work itself. It becomes concrete when finance counts how many contractor costs it reconciles by hand each month, or when a contractor asks to submit receipts the way employees do.
