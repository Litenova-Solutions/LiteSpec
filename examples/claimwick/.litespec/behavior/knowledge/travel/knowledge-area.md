# Travel

Travel is the Claimwick knowledge area that holds the rates for work travel: the daily allowance and the mileage rate for each country Claimwick covers. An employee who travels to a client depends on these rates to get the travel cost back, so the submission reads the row in force for the travel date and country.

## Variants

Travel varies by country, with one set per country: Germany and the Netherlands. Each set holds its own dated rows, so a rate change in one country leaves the other untouched. The rates are example figures that show the shape of the tables.

## Example

Ava travels to a client in Germany and stays one night: the submission reads the German per-diem row in force on the travel date from `reference-data/travel.per-diem-rates`.
