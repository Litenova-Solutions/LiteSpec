# Decision Target

Claimwick decides each expense within five business days of its submission. The table holds that target with the date it takes effect, so the approval rules cite one value instead of stating their own.

## Values

The one row holds 5 business days from 2026-01-01, resting on `research/approval-time-benchmarks`. A new row takes effect on its date, and the old row carries its end date.

## Example

An expense submitted on Monday is decided by the next Monday: the approval rule reads the target of five business days from this table.
