# Expenses Wait Weeks

## Situation

Submitted expenses wait weeks for a decision, so employees chase managers by email. Decisions arrive late without notice.

## Evidence

The benchmark in research/approval-time-benchmarks shows small companies decide within one week, while Claimwick averages three.
