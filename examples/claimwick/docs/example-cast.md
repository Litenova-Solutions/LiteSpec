# Example Cast

## The Organization

Harbor and Pine is a small consultancy of forty people. Employees travel to clients and file the cost.

## The People

Ava is an employee who travels. Ben is her manager and approver. Cara works in finance and runs reimbursements.

## The Occasion

A client visit in another city: train fare, one hotel night, and meals.

## The Amounts

The receipt threshold is 25 EUR. A train fare runs about 48.90 EUR.

## Recurring Situations

Ava submits with the receipt attached. Ben decides within days. Cara reimburses on Fridays.
