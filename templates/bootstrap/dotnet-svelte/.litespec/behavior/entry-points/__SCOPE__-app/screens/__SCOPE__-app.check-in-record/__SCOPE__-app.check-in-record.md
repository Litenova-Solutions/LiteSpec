# Check-in Record

The check-in record is where Ava reads one check-in she opened from the list. It shows the recorded moment, the note, and the state behind the row.

## Job

Ava reads the recorded state of the check-in she opened from the list.

## States

- Loading: the record shows as placeholders while the check-in loads.
- Empty: the record says the check-in does not exist.
- Failed: the record says the check-in is unavailable and offers to try again.

The record offers the read use case. It leads back to the check-in list.
