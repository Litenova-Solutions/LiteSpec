# Check-in List

The check-in list is where Ava finds a recorded check-in and where she records a new one. The rows show the newest check-in first with its recorded moment, its note, and its state, and the record action sits beside them.

## Job

Ava scans the recorded check-ins to find one, opens it, or records a new one.

## States

- Loading: the rows show as placeholders while the check-ins load.
- Empty: the list says no check-in is recorded yet and offers to record one.
- Failed: the list says the check-ins are unavailable and offers to try again.

The list offers the list and the record use cases. It leads to the check-in record and back home.
