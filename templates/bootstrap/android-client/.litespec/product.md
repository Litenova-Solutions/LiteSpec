# __PROJECT__

__PROJECT__ shows recorded health check-ins on the phone. Visitors open the list to find a recorded check-in and read its recorded state on the detail screen.

## Scope

Health shows the recorded check-ins in a list and one detail screen, reading them from the backend starter over HTTP.

## Users

Visitors who had a check-in recorded reach Health through the Android app. They open the list, read one check-in behind it, and step back.

## Out of Scope

The app records no check-in of its own; recording stays with the backend starter.

## Direction

Visitors read their recorded check-ins on the phone in any light.
