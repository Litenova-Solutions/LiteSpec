# Record Title

Say what message the person reads, when they read it and why it matters to them. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the notification from this paragraph.

## Content

Say what the person reads, in one or two sentences, and which facts the message must state.

## Delivery

Say when the message is sent, when it is suppressed and through which channel.

## Example

Write one concrete message for the example cast, with the real values filled in.
