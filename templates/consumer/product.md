# Record Title

Say what the product is in plain words, who it concerns and the consequence at stake. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the product from this paragraph.

## Scope

Say what the product does for its users, in one or two sentences.

## Users

Name who uses the product and what each of them does with it.

## Out of Scope

Name what the product does not do, and say why it stays out.

## Direction

Optional. Point to the vision records that say where the product is heading, and name the decision the direction depends on.
