# Record Title

Say what outside rule this is, in plain words, and who it binds. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the constraint from this paragraph.

## Scope

Say which subjects, transactions and periods the rule covers, and what it leaves out. Scope says whom the rule itself covers; the `applies` field and `appliesBecause` say whether it binds the product.

## Rules

Give each rule as its own paragraph, with its condition and the source that states it, cited as a reference link `[text][key]` to a key in the record's `sources`. State the outside obligation, not what the product does about it, and state no fact the source does not state as settled.

## Effect on the Product

Give each effect the rule has on the product as its own paragraph, and name by identifier each requirement that holds the effect. A constraint with `applies: false` says in one sentence that the rule binds nothing in the product, and why.

## Open Points

Optional. Say what is not settled about how a verified rule applies to the product, who will settle it and which behavior waits on it. A question about the facts of the rule belongs in the research record's Open Questions.
