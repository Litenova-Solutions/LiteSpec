# Record Title

Say what this runbook recovers or operates and when someone needs it. A reader who has read nothing else should know the task from this paragraph.

## Prerequisites

Say what the operator needs before starting: access, tools and the state the system must be in.

## Steps

Number each step. Give one action per step, with the exact command or screen.

## Verification

Say how to confirm the procedure worked.

## Recovery

Say what to do when a step fails, and how to return the system to its last good state.
