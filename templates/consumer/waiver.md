# Record Title

Say which provision this waiver sets aside and why the team does so. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the waiver from this paragraph.

## Provision

Name each provision, page or finding code the waiver sets aside, by its identifier.

## Reason

Say why the team sets the provision aside, in one or two sentences.

## Risk

Say what the waiver risks for the product and the people who use it.

## Expiry

State the date the waiver ends and what closes the gap. Say so when the waiver has no end, and why.
