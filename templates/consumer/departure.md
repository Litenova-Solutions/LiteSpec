# Record Title

Say which provision this departure sets aside and why the team does so. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the departure from this paragraph.

## Provision

Name each provision, page or finding code the departure sets aside, by its identifier.

## Reason

Say why the team sets the provision aside, in one or two sentences.

## Risk

Say what the departure risks for the product and the people who use it.

## Expiry

State the date the departure ends and what closes the gap. Say so when the departure has no end, and why.
