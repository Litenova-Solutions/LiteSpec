# Record Title

Say what outside system this is and why the product depends on it. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the dependency from this paragraph.

## Use

Say why the product depends on the system and which calls it makes.

## Data Shared

Say what data the product sends to this system and what the system sends back, and what trust the product places in each.

## Failure Handling

Say what an outage looks like for the person, and what the product does while the system is down.
