# Record Title

Say what the affected users cannot reliably do, the gap that causes it, and what they lose or risk as a result. A reader who has read nothing else should understand the problem from this paragraph.

## Affected Users

Name who has this problem and the circumstances in which they meet it. Describe the people the evidence covers, not a whole market.

## Failure Cases

- Give each failure that can happen on its own its own entry: what is missing or wrong, how the users would find it, and what happens when they do not.

## Example

Optional. Show one concrete case when the failure cases are hard to follow, and use a table when several cases compare.

## Current Workaround

Say what those users do today instead, and the burden it carries: the time it takes, what they repeat or must remember, and what it still misses.

## Cost

Say what the problem costs them, in their own units such as hours, money or lost sales, with time and money apart. Say whether each figure is measured or estimated, and write "unknown" when nobody has measured it.

## Evidence

Give the proof that the problem exists, with its source and its date, and say what each source shows. A file format, an API or another product shows that a solution is possible, not that the problem exists.

## Resolution Criteria

- Give one observable outcome for each failure case that shows it no longer happens. Name no feature, format or screen.
