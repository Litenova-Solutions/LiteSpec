Remaining Recommendations for the Seams

One medium finding and two lows remain open. Each is one small change with its evidence in the appendix.

1. Approve LiteSpec's own decisions

Ten provisions say Settled by a decision, but all nineteen decisions are still draft. Approve them before tagging, or say Proposed by. Finding 1.

2. Bring the book labels inside the model

The book page shows verdicts and proof labels the label rule does not allow. Add these states to the model and glossary, or relabel them. Finding 2.

3. Say that consumer findings carry levels

The command prints [G] and [A], but its page never states it. One sentence on the consumer page closes it. Finding 3.
