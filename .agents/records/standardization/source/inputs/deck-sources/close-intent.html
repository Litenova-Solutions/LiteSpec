Review

Recommendations for the Intent Layer

Ordered by what unblocks the most. Each step removes a class of findings rather than one finding.

1. Fix the two defects Lantern exposed

Draft glossary terms always fail draft-without-decider; a journey alone clears requirement-unfulfilled. Both are small code changes with a test. Findings 14, 15.

2. Close the mechanism gaps

Implement or demote: parent-changed flagging, aged questions becoming known gaps, the statement-number rule of requirement-unmeasured. Findings 1, 6, 10.

3. Settle the question lifecycle

Required fields (ballot; recommended on tier 3), a question status, the answer step, the veto, and whether records ever auto-approve. Findings 2, 4, 5, 7, 23, 24.

4. Simplify the hierarchy

The proposal on the previous slide: topic as parent, one list of reasons, generated constraint answers, four renames. Findings 16, 35 to 41, 47, 49.

5. Explain before prescribing

Add the questions table and the write-when and skip-when notes to intent.md; define decider, record layer, risk class and check tier in the glossary. Findings 12, 19, 31, 38.

6. Make schema, template and page agree

Validate templates in CI; document the quality fields; one approval date on decisions; owner and status on requirement pages; approve decisions 0001 to 0009. Findings 3, 11, 17, 33, 46.

Method and limits
