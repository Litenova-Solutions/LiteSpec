Review

Remaining Recommendations for the Intent Layer

Three low findings remain open. Each is one small change with its evidence in the appendix.

1. Link constraints to their research

A constraint carries its evidence only as free text in authority; the Links table names no constraint row. Add cites to the constraint, or state that free text is enough. Finding 1.

2. Close decision basedOn to product decisions

The schema lets any decision name requirements, while the pages allow it only on technical decisions. Restrict by home, or reword the pages. Finding 2.

3. Settle the container names

Topic is a record; settings, rules, entry points and permissions sit on -set and -list page styles. Use one suffix, or state why they differ. Finding 3.
